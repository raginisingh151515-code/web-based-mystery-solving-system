How an investigation works

Every action costs points, so think before you act. A sharp detective solves a case with fewer moves.

01

Read the briefing

Every case opens with a short report. Note the facts, the time and the place.

02

Search & question

Investigate locations and interrogate suspects to uncover hidden clues.

03

Build your board

All evidence collects on your case board. Keep private notes as you connect the dots.

04

Make an accusation

Name the culprit, method and motive. You get three attempts - choose carefully.
